Company Policy Document (Internal Use Only)

Document Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 17 May 2026
Classification: Confidential

1. Introduction

This document outlines the official workplace policies of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. It is designed to ensure a safe, productive, and professional environment for all employees, contractors, and interns.

All employees are required to follow the guidelines stated herein. Violation of these policies may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

2. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees are expected to work a total of 32 hours per week.
Flexible working hours may be granted upon manager approval.
Late arrivals beyond 30 minutes must be reported to HR.
3. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to team approval.
Remote workers must remain reachable during working hours.
Stable internet and secure workspace are mandatory.
Sensitive company data must not be accessed from public/shared devices.
4. Code of Conduct

All employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.

Prohibited behavior includes:

Harassment or discrimination of any kind
Sharing confidential company data externally
Misuse of company resources
Engaging in illegal or unethical activities

Respect, integrity, and accountability are core values of the company.

5. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per year.
Sick leave must be reported on the same day.
Planned leave must be requested at least 3 days in advance.
Excessive absenteeism may affect performance evaluation.
6. Data Security Policy
Employees must use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication where available.
Confidential data must not be shared via personal email or messaging apps.
All company data remains the property of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Any data breach must be reported immediately to the IT department.
7. Internet and Device Usage
Company internet is to be used for work-related purposes only.
Access to malicious, illegal, or non-work-related websites is prohibited.
Installing unauthorized software on company devices is not allowed.
IT team reserves the right to monitor device activity.
8. Performance Evaluation
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually.
Evaluation is based on productivity, teamwork, and goal achievement.
Employees may receive promotions, bonuses, or improvement plans based on performance.
9. Termination Policy

Employment may be terminated under the following conditions:

Repeated policy violations
Poor performance despite warnings
Breach of confidentiality agreement
Voluntary resignation with a minimum 2 weeks notice period
10. Amendments

Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to update or modify this policy at any time. Employees will be notified of major changes via official communication channels.

11. Acknowledgement

All employees must acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with this policy document.
